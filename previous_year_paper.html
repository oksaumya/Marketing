10. Case Study – Amul's Rural Market Expansion

Amul introduced smaller and affordable milk product packs in rural markets to attract middle-income consumers. The company also strengthened its distribution network through local retailers and village-level promotional campaigns. As a result, the brand improved customer reach and increased sales in semi-urban and rural areas.

Case questions:
  1. Identify and explain any two elements of the marketing mix used by Amul in this case. (4 Marks)
  2. Explain how effective distribution strategies helped Amul improve market penetration. (5 Marks)

11. Case Study – Coca-Cola's Localized Marketing

Coca-Cola customized its advertisements and packaging according to regional festivals and languages in India. During summer campaigns, the company introduced localized slogans and affordable pricing to attract customers from different regions.

Case Questions:
  1. Explain how localization supports Coca-Cola's marketing strategy in India. (4 Marks)
  2. Discuss the importance of pricing and promotion in increasing customer engagement during seasonal campaigns. (5 Marks)
