10. Case Study – Patanjali's Entry into FMCG

Patanjali entered the highly competitive FMCG market by positioning itself as a swadeshi, Ayurvedic, and affordable alternative to MNC brands like HUL and Colgate. It used Baba Ramdev as a brand ambassador, kept prices 15–30% lower than competitors, and distributed through both modern retail and small local stores. Within five years, it captured significant share in the toothpaste, ghee, and shampoo categories.

Case questions:
  1. Identify and explain any two elements of the marketing mix used by Patanjali in this case. (4 Marks)
  2. Explain how Patanjali used segmentation, targeting, and positioning (STP) to differentiate itself from MNC competitors. (5 Marks)

11. Case Study – Zomato's Customer Retention Strategy

Zomato introduced "Zomato Gold" — a paid membership that offers free delivery on orders above ₹199, additional discounts at partner restaurants, and gamified rewards for frequent users. The company also uses data from past orders to recommend new restaurants and dishes to each user. As a result, repeat orders from Gold members have increased significantly.

Case questions:
  1. Identify the type of loyalty programme used by Zomato and explain any two benefits it offers to customers. (4 Marks)
  2. Explain how personalisation and customer data can improve customer retention and lifetime value for digital platforms like Zomato. (5 Marks)

12. Case Study – Mamaearth's D2C Growth

Mamaearth started as a Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) skincare brand selling through its own website and Instagram. It focused on toxin-free, natural products and used influencer collaborations heavily. Within a few years, the brand also entered modern retail outlets (Nykaa, Amazon) and physical stores, becoming one of India's leading skincare unicorns.

Case questions:
  1. Identify and explain any two promotional strategies used by Mamaearth to build brand awareness. (4 Marks)
  2. Discuss the advantages and challenges of moving from a D2C model to an omnichannel distribution strategy. (5 Marks)

Part C — Case skeletons

10. Patanjali — (a) Price (15–30% lower); Promotion (Baba Ramdev brand ambassador, swadeshi appeal). (b) Segmentation: psychographic (Ayurveda believers, swadeshi sentiment). Targeting: middle-class Indian household. Positioning: "Affordable, natural, swadeshi alternative" — clear value vs MNC.

11. Zomato — (a) Paid subscription / tiered loyalty. Benefits: free delivery, exclusive discounts, gamified rewards. (b) Personalisation increases relevance → higher repeat orders → higher CLV. Data enables targeted offers, predictive recommendations, reducing churn.

12. Mamaearth — (a) Influencer marketing on Instagram; Content-led digital storytelling (toxin-free narrative). (b) Advantages of omnichannel: wider reach, offline trust, more touchpoints. Challenges: channel conflict, inventory sync, brand consistency, margin pressure.
