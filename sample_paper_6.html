10. Case Study – Amazon Prime's Subscription Loyalty

Amazon Prime, launched in India in 2016, bundles free fast delivery, Prime Video (movies and series), Prime Music, and exclusive deals into a single annual subscription. Members spend 2–3x more than non-members and have higher retention rates. Amazon uses purchase history to personalise recommendations and exclusive sale access for Prime members.

Case questions:
  1. Identify the type of loyalty programme used by Amazon Prime and explain any two benefits it gives to customers. (4 Marks)
  2. Explain how Amazon Prime uses bundling, personalisation, and Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) to retain customers and increase profitability. (5 Marks)

11. Case Study – Swiggy Instamart vs Local Kirana Stores

Swiggy Instamart promises 10–15 minute grocery delivery in major Indian cities. It uses a network of dark stores located within neighbourhoods, dynamic pricing, and aggressive offers. Many local kirana stores have started losing footfall, while modern retailers (BigBasket, JioMart) are also responding with quick-delivery models.

Case questions:
  1. Identify and explain any two elements of the marketing mix that give Swiggy Instamart its competitive advantage. (4 Marks)
  2. Discuss the impact of quick-commerce on traditional retail and suggest two strategies kirana stores can adopt to stay competitive. (5 Marks)

12. Case Study – Boat Lifestyle's Influencer-Driven Growth

Boat, an Indian audio brand, focused on affordable wireless earphones and headphones priced 30–50% lower than international competitors. Instead of celebrity endorsements, it partnered with hundreds of micro-influencers, cricketers, and YouTubers to build the brand. Today, Boat is one of the top-selling earwear brands in India and competes with Sony, JBL, and Apple.

Case questions:
  1. Identify the segmentation and positioning approach used by Boat. (4 Marks)
  2. Explain how Boat used digital promotion, influencer marketing, and pricing strategy to differentiate itself from MNC brands. (5 Marks)

Part C — Case skeletons

10. Amazon Prime — (a) Paid subscription loyalty. Benefits: free fast delivery, video/music streaming, exclusive deals. (b) Bundling raises perceived value; Personalisation (recommendations) → higher relevance → more purchases. CLV: Prime members buy 2–3x → higher lifetime profit per customer covers acquisition cost.

11. Swiggy Instamart — (a) Place (dark stores hyperlocal → 10-min delivery); Price (dynamic + offers). (b) Impact on kirana: reduced footfall on commodity items, but still hold ground on credit + relationships. Kirana strategies: tie up with delivery apps (DotPe, Swiggy), introduce phone/WhatsApp ordering, focus on personalised service and fresh produce.

12. Boat — (a) Segmentation: demographic (youth 16–30) + psychographic (music lovers, value-conscious). Positioning: "Premium-looking audio at affordable prices for young India". (b) Digital promotion + influencer army (instead of expensive celebrity); aggressive pricing (penetration); product design language that looks premium; cricket sponsorships → mass aspirational reach without MNC budget.
